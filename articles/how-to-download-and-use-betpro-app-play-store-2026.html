Make Your Account

When you open the app for the first time, tap Sign Up. Type in your mobile number and choose a strong password. Do not use your birthday or "123456". You will get a code by SMS. Type that code in, and your account is ready. Keep your password private. Nobody from BetPro will ever ask you for it.

The Dashboard

After you log in, you will see the dashboard. This is the home screen of the app. You can see your balance here, open the menu, and reach deposit, withdrawal and notifications. Take a minute to tap around and get comfortable. Just looking does not cost you anything.

How to Deposit

To start, you need to add money to your account. This is called a deposit. Open the Deposit option from the dashboard or menu.

Choose the payment option that is easiest for you and type the amount. Start with a small amount the first time. After you pay, the app will ask for a few details, like your transaction ID, so it can match the payment to your account. Fill them in carefully and tap Submit.

Then wait a little for your balance to update. If it does not show up, do not send the money again. Contact support with your transaction ID instead. Also, only use the payment details shown inside the app. Never send money to a number someone gives you in a chat.

How to Withdraw

Open the menu and tap Withdraw (or Request Withdrawal). Type the amount, add the account where you want to receive your money, and check every number twice. One wrong digit can send your money to the wrong place. Then tap Submit.

Your request will be checked first and then sent to you once it is approved. This can take a little time, so please be patient. It also helps to use an account in your own name, because a different name can cause delays.

Notifications

Do not skip this part. The notifications page tells you when your deposit is approved and when your withdrawal is on its way. Turn notifications on in your phone settings so you do not miss anything.

Tips to Stay Safe

  • If you lose, do not bet more to win it back.
  • Never share your login with anyone, not even someone who says they are from support.
  • Keep the app updated from the Play Store and lock your phone with a PIN or fingerprint.
  • Take breaks. If it stops being fun, stop.

If the app does not install, check your free space and internet, then restart your phone and try again. If you do not get the SMS code, wait a minute and ask for a new one. If you forget your password, use the Forgot Password option on the login screen.
