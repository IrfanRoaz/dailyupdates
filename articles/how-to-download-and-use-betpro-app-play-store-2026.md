## How to Download BetPro from the Play Store

This is the easy part. It takes about two minutes.

**Step 1.** Open the **Google Play Store** on your phone.

**Step 2.** Tap the search bar at the top and type **BetPro**.

**Step 3.** Look for the right app in the results. Check the name carefully. Fake apps with similar names exist, so do not tap the first thing you see without looking.

**Step 4.** Tap **Install** and wait. When it finishes, the button changes to **Open**.

**Step 5.** Tap **Open**, or find the BetPro icon on your home screen.

That's it. The app is on your phone.

> **Tip:** If you cannot find the app in your Play Store, do not panic and do not download it from random websites or WhatsApp links. Files from unknown places can steal your information. Only use the official source.

## How to Create Your Account

When you open the app for the first time, it will ask you to sign up. Here is what to do:

1. Tap **Sign Up** or **Register**.
2. Enter your **mobile number**.
3. Make a **password**. Choose something strong that only you know. Do not use your birthday or "123456".
4. You will get a **code by SMS**. Type it in to confirm your number.
5. Tap **Continue**, and your account is ready.

Write your password somewhere safe, or save it in your phone's password manager. And never share it with anyone, even if they say they work for BetPro. Real support staff will never ask for your password.

Type the amount you want and add the account where you want to receive it. Check every number twice. One wrong digit can send your money to the wrong place.

### Step 3: Send the request

Tap **Submit** to send your request.

![Request a withdrawal in BetPro](https://betproapp.co/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/betpro-request-withdrawal.webp)

### Step 4: Wait for it to be approved

Your request will be checked first. Once it is approved, the money is sent to you. This can take a little time, so be patient. If it takes much longer than expected, contact support.

**Good to know:** Use the same name on your withdrawal account as the name on your BetPro account. If the names do not match, it can cause delays.

2. **Never chase losses.** If you lose, do not try to win it back by betting more. This is how people get into trouble.
3. **Do not share your login.** Not with friends, not with family, not with "support" on WhatsApp.
4. **Update the app** from the Play Store when a new version comes out.
5. **Lock your phone** with a PIN or fingerprint.
6. **Take breaks.** If it stops being fun, stop.

If betting starts to hurt your family, your work, or your peace of mind, please step away and talk to someone you trust.

## Common Problems and Easy Fixes

**The app will not install.**
Check your free space and your internet. Restart your phone and try again.

**The app is slow or keeps closing.**
Close other apps running in the background. Update the app. If it still does not work, uninstall it and install it again from the Play Store.

**I did not get the SMS code.**
Wait a minute, then ask for a new code. Check that your phone number is typed correctly and that you have signal.

**My deposit is not showing.**
Wait a little first. If it is still missing, contact support with your transaction ID and a screenshot.

**I forgot my password.**
Use the **Forgot Password** option on the login screen and follow the steps.
